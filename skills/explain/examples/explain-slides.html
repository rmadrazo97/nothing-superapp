08 · the standard
a house style

Rules the output never breaks.

01
Vanilla HTML.
Zero dependencies. One file. Opens on file://.
02
Inline SVG diagrams.
No CDN. No Mermaid unless asked. Works offline forever.
03
PDF-ready.
A real @media print block. Cmd-P is a first-class output.
04
Reads your tokens.
Auto-detects design-system/styles.css and inherits your brand.
05
No AI slop.
No purple gradients. No emoji headers. No rounded-XL cards with drop shadows.
06
Shareable by default.
One file to email, screenshot, deploy, or drop in a repo.
