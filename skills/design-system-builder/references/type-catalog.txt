# Type Catalog — Curated Font Pairings

All entries below are **Google Fonts** (free, CDN-hostable, no license worry) or have a canonical Google Fonts fallback. Load via `@import url(...)` at the top of `styles.css`.

## Rules

- **Two families max per surface.** Display + text. A monospace accessory is allowed but should not exceed 20% of any given screen.
- **Same family for display + text is the safest ship.** Use two weights (500 heading / 400 body) and let hierarchy come from size.
- **Google Fonts fallbacks are mandatory when the primary is paid.** Ship the free fallback; upgrade later.
- **`@import` at the top of `styles.css`, weights only what you use.** Extra weights add ~40KB each.

## Heading weight defaults per direction

| Direction | Heading weight | Body weight |
|---|---|---|
| Serif — editorial | 400 or 600 | 400 |
| Sans grotesque | 500 or 600 | 400 |
| Sans humanist | 500 or 700 | 400 |
| Display + text | display: 400 · text: 500 for headings, 400 for body |

Set the chosen weight in `--font-heading-weight` in `styles.css`.
